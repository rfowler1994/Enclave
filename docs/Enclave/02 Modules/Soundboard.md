A [[Modules|module]] that allows the DM to play and control music, ambience, and sound effects during a session.
# Core Features
- Ability to add and remove local audio files from the soundboard.
- Ability to save configured sounds for reuse across sessions.
- Ability to play, pause, and stop individual sounds.
- Ability to play multiple sounds simultaneously.
- Independent volume controls for each playing sound.
- Ability to configure individual sounds to loop continuously.
- Ability to stop all currently playing sounds at once.
- Audio playback continues when Soundboard is hidden or inactive, including when switching layouts, but stops when the module is closed.
# Potential Features
- Ability to organize sounds into categories or groups, such as music, ambience, and sound effects.
- Ability to fade sounds in or out rather than starting or stopping abruptly.
- Integration with external music services, such as Spotify.
- Ability to assign keyboard shortcuts to frequently used sounds.
